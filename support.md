## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device only. Aura has no accounts, no server, and no analytics. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**How do I back up my data?**
Aura does not currently sync or back up. Use Export in Settings to save a PDF or CSV copy of your history.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings > Delete all data. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Is Aura medical advice?**
No. Aura is a tracking tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor about your migraines.
