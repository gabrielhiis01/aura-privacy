Inside Aura

Log with one tap

Start an attack log with one tap. Everything else can wait until you can look at a screen.

Learn what helps

Aura follows up after you log a medication and asks whether it helped. Over time, you can see which of your medications actually work for you.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no ads.
